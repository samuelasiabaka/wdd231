Choose the membership level that fits your organization, then submit the application form. The options below follow the assignment's required Non-Profit, Silver, and Gold structure.

Non-Profit

Community

Silver

Growth

For businesses seeking stronger visibility and professional development.

  • Everything in Non-Profit
  • Training opportunities
  • Social media features
  • Business luncheons
  • Enhanced directory profile

Gold

Leadership

For organizations seeking deeper visibility, connections, and event participation.

  • Everything in Silver
  • Priority event opportunities
  • Sponsorship opportunities
  • Advertising opportunities
